Signs a drain needs cleaning, not another plunge

One slow drain is usually a local clog. Several slow drains at once point to the branch or main line, and that changes the tool we bring.

  • Water gurgles in a tub or toilet when the washing machine drains.
  • Two or more fixtures slow down on the same day.
  • A drain clears for a week, then slows again.
  • A sewer smell rises from a floor drain or basement cleanout.
  • The toilet bubbles or water backs into the shower after a flush.

What clogs drains in Easley homes

Easley grew as a railroad and textile town, so many homes near downtown and along Pendleton Street are decades old. Older drain lines can have rough cast iron walls where grease and soap scum cling. A line like that clogs more often than smooth PVC.

Newer homes toward Powdersville and the US-123 corridor have different problems. Builders' debris, wipes and kitchen grease cause most of the calls there. In both cases the clog is only half the story, because the pipe's condition decides how long a cleaning will last.

Kitchen sink clogs and the garbage disposal

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathrooms. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and hardens into a ring that narrows the line a little more with every dishwasher cycle. Coffee grounds and rice finish the job.

If your disposal hums but water will not leave the sink, the blockage may sit past the disposal. We clear that run with a cable, then check the disposal itself. Stuck or leaking units are covered on our garbage disposal repair page.

Why a drain keeps clogging after snaking

A cable punches a hole through a clog but leaves the coating on the pipe wall. If the same drain slows again within weeks, the wall is the problem. Roots, a bellied section of pipe or heavy scale can all cause it.

That is when we pull a camera through the line. Depending on what we see, the next step is jetting, a spot repair or, for a damaged line, sewer line repair. You see the footage before approving anything.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Do this while you wait for the plumber

.

Treat it as an emergency when sewage is coming up in a tub or floor drain, when every fixture is backing up, or when water is spreading toward carpet or a finished basement. Raw sewage is a health hazard, so keep children and pets out of the area.

  1. Stop using every sink, shower, toilet and appliance that drains.
  2. Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a backed-up line. It can burn skin and make the plumber's work riskier.
  3. Move rugs and stored items away from the affected drain.
  4. Open a window if there is a sewer smell and keep the area ventilated.
  5. Take a quick photo of any overflow for your records, then call our 24/7 line.

If the backup came through a floor drain at the lowest level of the house, tell us when you call. That usually points to the main line rather than a single fixture.

Drain line leaks and slow seepage

Not every wet spot comes from a clog. A leaking drain joint under a tub or a cracked line under the floor can mimic a slow drain and add moisture to the wall or subfloor. We check for a leak before and after cleaning, and the camera helps trace it.

What to do while you wait for the plumber

The steps are simple, and they keep a bad clog from turning into a wet floor.

  • Stop running water into the clogged fixture and the ones that share its line.
  • Do not pour chemical drain openers. They sit in the pipe and burn the next person who opens the trap.
  • Put a bucket or towels around the fixture if water is rising.
  • Check whether other drains in the house are slow, since that points to a main line.
  • Note the last time the drain was cleaned and what was happening when the problem started.

Floor drains, laundry lines and basements

Not every clog is in a kitchen or a bathroom. Laundry standpipes overflow when lint and soap build up, and floor drains in a garage or utility room back up when the line behind them narrows. These lines are often shorter and more exposed, so a cable usually clears them fast.

If a floor drain backs up whenever the washer drains, the washer line shares a branch with it. A cleaning at the cleanout fixes both, and a lint trap on the washer hose keeps the problem from coming back.

For a basement or crawlspace that shows water near a drain, tell us on the phone. It can point to a main line problem, which is covered on our sewer line cleaning page.

What the plumbing code says about drains

Drain pipe sizing, slope and venting are set by the IPC, which South Carolina adopts through its Building Codes Council with state changes. That is why we check slope and vent paths when a drain keeps slowing, and why a cleaning that finds a sag or a missing vent leads to a repair quote instead of another cleaning.
