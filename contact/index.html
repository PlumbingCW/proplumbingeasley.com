What to have ready when you call

A few details speed up dispatch.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross road.
  • What you see: where water is, what is not working, how long it has been going on.
  • Whether you have shut off the main valve.
  • Whether you rent or own, since that affects who approves the work.
  • A good number to reach you if the call drops.

When to call someone else first

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. If sewage or flood water is near live electrical equipment, stay out and call 911. We can help once the area is safe.
