About Glenwood in Easley

Glenwood sits just off downtown Easley, around Front Street, Blue Ridge Street, Barton Street, Garrison Street, Day Street, League Street, Turner Street, Hagood Street and Glenwood Road. Some listing sites label part of the area Glenwood Mill. We found no official boundary map, so we describe it by these streets and do not draw lines.

Residents are close to the Main Street district and the Doodle Trail, a 7.5 mile paved path on the old railroad route that opened in 2015. That walkable setting is part of why people stay in these older houses and keep them up.

What the homes look like

Public listing data puts the median build year near 1920, with sold homes ranging from 1902 into the 2020s. Most are single-family bungalows and renovated houses on lots of about a quarter acre, often with porches and detached garages or carports.

For plumbing, the useful point is simple. A street where houses were built and remodeled across a century can hold more than one generation of pipe and fixtures. We do not guess what is behind your walls. We look, explain what we see and give you a price before work begins.

Water and sewer in Easley

Easley Combined Utilities provides water and sewer service in Easley. In 2026 it announced a multi-phase sewer project on the Brushy Creek, 18 Mile Creek and Middle Branch interceptors, which it describes as aimed at old, leaking clay pipes. The project names no neighborhoods, so we do not claim it affects Glenwood.

If a backup might be in the street main and not in your house, call the utility office at 864-859-4013 to ask. If the problem is inside your property line, a camera inspection shows the pipe before anyone digs.

Simple habits for older homes

These steps help any house, whatever its age.

  • Find the main shutoff and make sure it turns before an emergency.
  • Look under sinks and around the water heater twice a year for damp spots.
  • Keep grease and wipes out of the drains.
  • Note when a slow drain started and which fixtures share it.
  • Ask for a written price before any work begins.
