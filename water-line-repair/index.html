What the water service line is

The water line begins at the utility's meter or tap and runs underground to your house. The meter and the part up to it belong to the utility. The pipe from the meter to your home is typically the homeowner's responsibility, though that boundary varies by utility.

Easley Combined Utilities serves water in the city. Ask where its responsibility ends on your street so you know who handles what. For homes in Powdersville, the water provider is a separate district, so the boundary may differ.

Signs of a failing service line

Underground leaks can run for weeks before you notice.

  • A wet or unusually green patch in the yard along the line.
  • A meter that turns with all fixtures off.
  • A rising water bill with no change in use.
  • Low pressure throughout the house.
  • Muddy or discolored water.
  • The sound of running water near the meter box.

When a water line leak is an emergency

Protect the house and the yard

.

A broken service line can send thousands of gallons into the ground, undermine a driveway and drop pressure to the house.

  1. Close the shutoff on your side of the meter, often at the house or in the meter box.
  2. If you cannot close it, call the utility to shut off at the meter.
  3. Keep people away from soft or sinking ground near the leak.
  4. Do not dig in the yard until we mark underground utilities.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and describe where water is surfacing.

In South Carolina, call 811 before any digging so underground utilities are marked.

High bills without a visible leak

A buried leak does not always show on the surface. The soil can absorb water for weeks. A rising bill and a moving meter are often the only clues.

Start with a meter test and an indoor check of toilets and irrigation, then call us if the dial still moves. Our leak detection page explains how we trace it.

Pressure problems and regulators

Many homes have a pressure regulator where the line enters. A failed regulator causes too much or too little pressure, both of which damage fixtures. We test pressure at a hose bib and replace regulators as needed.

Low pressure from a corroded line may also point to repiping or line replacement.

What happens during a service line repair

The sequence is the same for most jobs.

  1. Close the shutoff on your side of the meter and confirm the leak is on your line.
  2. Call 811 so underground utilities are marked.
  3. Locate the leak and decide between a spot repair and a longer replacement.
  4. Excavate, repair or replace and pressure test the line.
  5. Backfill, restore the surface and check the meter for movement.

Pipe materials in service lines

Service lines come in several materials. Older houses may have galvanized steel, which rusts, or copper, which can pinhole. Newer lines use PEX or polyethylene, which are flexible and resist corrosion. The right choice depends on the soil, the length and the local requirements.

We choose a material that meets the code and lasts. If the line connects to a pressure regulator, we check the regulator at the same time, because high pressure shortens the life of any pipe.

Underground utility rules for service lines

South Carolina law requires a call to 811 before digging so underground utilities are marked. Drinking water pipe and fittings should meet the NSF/ANSI 61 standard, and the plumbing code governs the line from the meter to the house.
