Toto Toilet Installation Cost in Easley, SC

.

Installing a Toto toilet is usually a one-to-two hour job when the floor flange and shutoff valve are in good shape. The price depends on the toilet model you choose and on what the plumber finds when the old toilet comes up.

What affects the price

The toilet itself is the biggest part of the cost. Toto makes models that range from basic two-piece units to one-piece and wall-hung designs with built-in features. Installation labor varies less than the fixture price, but access, floor condition and the plumbing under the toilet can add to it.

Ask for the toilet price and the labor price separately. If you buy the toilet yourself, make sure it fits the rough-in, which is the distance from the wall to the center of the drain.

Steps in a typical installation

The sequence is short.

  1. Shut off the water, flush and sponge out the old tank and bowl.
  2. Disconnect the supply line and unbolt the old toilet.
  3. Lift it off, scrape the old wax and inspect the flange.
  4. Set a new wax ring or seal and lower the new toilet onto the flange.
  5. Tighten the bolts evenly, cut and cap them.
  6. Connect the supply line, fill the tank and test for leaks.
  7. Caulk the base if the local practice calls for it.

Why the flange matters

The flange is the ring on the floor that the toilet bolts to. If it is cracked or sits below the finished floor, the toilet can rock or leak at the base. A repair ring or a new flange may be needed, and it adds to the price.

A toilet that rocks, or a stain around the base, is also covered under toilet repair. If the floor is soft, ask about repair before the new toilet goes on.

Choosing a model

Look at flush rating, bowl shape and height. An elongated bowl is more comfortable for most adults, and a comfort-height seat is easier for older users. Water use matters, too. Many modern units use 1.28 gallons per flush or less.

If you are remodeling a bathroom, tell your plumber early. A new toilet may need the drain moved or a wall-hung carrier. See plumbing installation for how that planning works.

Wall-hung, one-piece and two-piece differences

A two-piece toilet has a separate tank and bowl, and it is the easiest to carry and install. A one-piece unit is heavier but easier to clean and often sits lower. A wall-hung toilet mounts to a carrier inside the wall, which changes the framing and the plumbing behind it.

A wall-hung unit is a remodel decision, not a swap. It needs a carrier rated for the weight, a wall deep enough to hold it and access for the supply and drain. If you are replacing a floor-mounted toilet with a wall-hung one, the quote will include the wall work.

Whatever type you choose, confirm the rough-in and the bolt spacing before you buy, and keep the manual and the warranty card with your records.

Questions to ask before you buy

The right questions prevent a return trip.

  • What is the rough-in size, and does the model match it?
  • Is the toilet round or elongated, and does the space allow it?
  • Does the price include the seat?
  • What is the flush volume and the warranty on the china and parts?
  • Do you want the old toilet hauled away?

Flush volume and installation standards

Federal rules cap toilet flush volume at 1.6 gallons, and EPA WaterSense labels models at 1.28 gallons or less. Installation follows the manufacturer's instructions and the IPC as adopted in South Carolina.

What this means in Easley, SC

In Easley, many older houses near downtown have older flanges and shutoff valves that have not been touched in decades. Replacing a toilet is a good time to replace those too. Newer subdivisions usually have newer parts, so the job is quicker.

Easley water is soft, with an average hardness of 6 mg/L in the 2023 utility report, so a new toilet is less likely to develop mineral stains in the bowl than in hard-water areas. Check the manufacturer's flush rating and warranty, and ask the City of Easley building department if your address needs a permit for plumbing changes beyond a like-for-like swap.
