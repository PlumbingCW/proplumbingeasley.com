Older streets, older pipes

Pickens grew around a courthouse and later along the Pickens Railway, which began running between Easley and Pickens in 1898. Houses on the streets near downtown and Pendleton Street reflect that long history, and many predate the subdivisions that cover the rest of the county. An older house often means clay or cast iron sewer lines and galvanized supply pipe.

That matters because both materials fail slowly. Clay joints let roots in, cast iron scales on the inside, and galvanized pipe rusts from the inside out. If your Pickens house is older, a camera look is usually cheaper than guessing.

Sewer backups in Pickens

The City of Pickens Utility Office handles water, sewer and sanitation. If the problem is in the city main in the street, the city has to fix it. If it is in the line between your house and the main, the repair is yours. A camera shows which side the break sits on.

Our sewer inspection page explains what the camera records, and sewer line cleaning covers clearing a blocked main line. For stubborn root growth we use hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall rather than just poking a hole through the blockage.

Places in Pickens that help with directions

City Hall is at 219 Pendleton Street, and a good number of our calls use it as a reference point. Hagood Mill, built in 1845, sits three miles north of downtown on US 178, and Table Rock State Park lies just beyond. Old Pickens Jail, built in 1903, is now a museum near the center of town.

The Doodle Trail covers 8.5 miles between Pickens and Easley on the old rail bed. Homes along that corridor sit on the same ground we cross when we drive from our Easley base, so the trip is short.

Higher ground and cooler mornings

Pickens sits at about 1,053 feet, a little higher than Easley. In a hard freeze, exposed pipes in crawlspaces and garages are the first to go, and houses with long supply runs under the floor need insulation. A dripping faucet during the coldest night can keep water moving.

If a pipe does burst, shut off the main and call us. Our burst pipe repair page walks through what the repair includes and how to limit the damage until we arrive.

Pickens homeowner checklist

Older houses reward a little regular attention.

  • Ask for a camera look at the sewer line if the house is older than the 1970s.
  • Locate your sewer cleanout and keep it clear.
  • Note which pipes in the crawlspace are copper, plastic or galvanized.
  • Insulate exposed lines before the first hard freeze.
  • Call the City of Pickens Utility Office for anything in the street, and call us for anything on your side.
