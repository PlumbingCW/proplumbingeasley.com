About Montague Lakes

Montague Lakes is in the 29640 ZIP code, about five minutes from downtown Easley according to listing data. Streets include Montague Drive, Upper Lake Drive, Warren Drive, New Haven Court, Elliot Lane and Barnett Way.

The community has a small fishing lake, walking trails, a pool and a clubhouse. Nearby schools are commonly listed as East End Elementary, Gettys Middle and Easley High. School assignment depends on the address.

Newer homes, different plumbing questions

Listing data shows homes from the late 1990s into 2025, with a median near 2004. Most are brick or craftsman style with garages, around four bedrooms and two and a half baths. Lots average more than half an acre.

More bathrooms and bigger lots change the questions. Hot water has to reach farther. Several people may use showers at the same time. Outdoor spigots and irrigation tie into the same supply. We talk about demand, not only the age of the equipment.

Checks for a larger home

A few quick looks can prevent surprises.

Upstairs bathrooms.

Watch for stains on the ceiling below a bathroom. Early staining can mean a slow leak, so see leak detection.

Outdoor spigots.

Disconnect hoses in cold weather and check for a drip after use. Faucet repair covers fixtures inside and out.

Water heater.

Look for moisture at the base and listen for popping. Water heater repair starts with a check.

Fixtures.

A toilet that runs or a faucet that drips wastes water quietly. Plumbing installation covers upgrades.
