What downtown Easley is

Easley was chartered in 1873 and grew around a railroad that made it a textile town. Trains still run along Main Street, and the old rail line became the 7.5 mile Doodle Trail, which opened in 2015.

Today downtown has shops, restaurants and bars, Old Market Square with its clock tower and amphitheater, Easley Park on Pendleton Street and the Easley Farmers Market on North First Street on Saturday mornings and Tuesday evenings in season. This page is about the commercial and mixed-use core, not a residential subdivision.

Why busy days stress plumbing

A restaurant or bar depends on drains and restrooms working while the room is full. Market days and concerts bring more people through the same fixtures. A slow floor drain or a running toilet is more than an annoyance when customers are waiting.

We cannot tell you which downtown building has which pipe. What we can do is look at your fixtures and explain what is happening before anyone commits to a repair.

Grease, drains and the rules that apply

Food businesses often have rules about grease. We do not know which rules apply to your building. Ask the City of Easley or Easley Combined Utilities what is required, then we can talk about maintenance, jetting or repairs that fit.

A camera inspection can show what is in a line before anyone cuts into a floor. See sewer camera inspection for how that works.

A short checklist for business owners

  • Know where the main water shutoff is and tell your staff.
  • Keep a short list of emergency contacts by the phone.
  • Do not pour grease into floor drains or sinks.
  • Report slow drains early, before a busy day.
  • Ask for the scope and the price in writing before work starts.
