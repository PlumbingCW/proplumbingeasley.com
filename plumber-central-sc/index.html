Downtown Central and the railroad

Central was founded by the Atlanta and Richmond Air Line Railway during the railroad boom that began in 1873, and the name reflects its spot midway between Atlanta and Charlotte. The rail line still runs through downtown. Homes near it include older houses with galvanized lines and old cast iron drains.

Near the tracks you will also find landmarks that help with directions: Madden Bridge Road, where the Central Roller Mills sit, and Church Street, home to Central High School and the Morgan House. Both of those historic buildings are on the National Register of Historic Places.

Student rentals and heavy use

Clemson University students rent apartments and houses in Central, and Southern Wesleyan University's main campus sits east of downtown. Rentals put heavy use on drains, disposals and water heaters, and a clog at 11 p.m. before a landlord can respond is a common call.

If you manage a rental or live in one, keep our drain cleaning, garbage disposal and toilet repair pages in mind. We coordinate with landlords and property managers so the repair is documented.

Where Central's water comes from

The Town of Central's 2024 water quality report says it uses water from Lake Hartwell, bought from the City of Clemson, and from Twelve-Mile Creek, bought from the Easley-Central water district. The lead results in that report stayed below the action level.

That is a water supply fact, not a pipe fact. Old galvanized pipe can still shed rust into good water. If your tap runs brown after sitting, ask us to check the supply line rather than assuming it is the utility.

Central renter and landlord checklist

Rentals turn over, and plumbing should be checked at each turnover.

  • Test every drain and toilet before a new tenant moves in.
  • Show tenants where the main shutoff and the water heater are.
  • Replace flappers and fill valves that are older than a few years.
  • Check under sinks for slow leaks and stained cabinet floors.
  • Keep a written record of repairs, with photos, for the lease file.
