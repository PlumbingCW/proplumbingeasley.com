Where Rollingwood is

Listings place Rollingwood near the Easley downtown core, a few minutes from downtown shops, with Greenville Highway among the streets named. Anchor streets include Rollingwood Drive, Rollingwood Way, Pinetree Lane, Onyx Court, Woodhaven Road and Smith Grove Road. We found no official boundary, so these streets are the best guide.

Local listings commonly pair the area with West End Elementary, Gettys Middle and Easley High, though school assignment depends on the address and should be confirmed with the district.

The houses and lots

Listing data shows homes built from 1967 into the early 2000s, with a median near 1976. Many are brick ranches and split-levels, some with basements, crawlspace storage, workshops and oversized garages. Lots are often half an acre or more.

Bigger lots and mixed foundations change where plumbing problems hide. A crawlspace can hide a slow drip. A long yard run can hide a wet patch. A garage can hold the water heater. We check the spots that fit your house.

Where plumbing hides in a ranch or split-level

A quick walk through these places often finds the cause.

Crawlspace or basement.

Look for damp soil, a musty smell or drips on pipes. See leak detection if you find moisture.

Garage water heater.

Check the floor around the tank and the relief valve line. A tank that is leaking at the bottom usually needs replacement.

Yard and outdoor spigots.

A soft, wet patch with no rain can mean a supply leak. Water line repair covers the line to the house.

Kitchen and bath drains.

Slow drains on one branch point to a local clog. Several at once call for drain cleaning.

Water heaters in homes of this era

A water heater installed years ago may be near the end of its working life, but age is only one clue. Rust at the base, water on the floor, lukewarm showers and noisy heating all matter. We look at the tank and tell you whether a repair makes sense.

If replacement is the better path, we explain tank and tankless options in plain terms. See water heater installation and tankless water heaters for what is involved.

Questions to ask any plumber

  • What did you find and how do you know?
  • Is the price for the whole job or only the first step?
  • What happens if the problem is different once the wall or floor is open?
  • Will you put the scope in writing first?
  • What should I watch for after the repair?
