Easley and the ZIP codes inside it

Easley uses ZIP codes 29640, 29641 and 29642. Most of the city is in Pickens County, and a small part is in Anderson County. City water comes from Easley Combined Utilities, and the Saluda Lake source and soft water make fixture scale a minor issue here.

We handle every plumbing service in Easley, from drain cleaning to water heater replacement. Our dispatch address is 407 Ross Ave.

How dispatch works across the area

All calls go to one 24/7 number, (888) 910-8644. We send the nearest available plumber and give you an arrival window when you call. Towns closer to Easley, such as Powdersville, are usually quicker than towns farther out, such as Central, and we say so on the phone.

Nights, weekends and holidays use the same line. The price is stated before work starts, and we do not add a surcharge for the hour without telling you first.

What differs between towns

Powdersville is a growing suburb with newer houses and water from the Powdersville Water District. Pickens is the county seat with older streets and city sewer. Liberty has mill-town housing and buys water from the Greenville Water System and the Easley-Central Water Department. Central is next to Clemson with rental homes and an older downtown.

Those differences change the first thing we check. Newer suburbs lean toward water heaters and hidden leaks. Older streets lean toward sewer lines and galvanized supply pipe.

Reading the map: how the five areas connect

Easley sits near the middle of the map. Powdersville lies to the south along I-85 and the Saluda River, and it is the quickest town for us to reach. Pickens is northwest on the old Doodle Line corridor, and the Doodle Trail follows that route for 8.5 miles. Liberty and Central run west along US 178 and toward Clemson.

That geography shapes dispatch. A call from Powdersville or Pickens is usually a short drive from our Easley base. A call from Central is longer, because it sits beyond Liberty near Clemson. When you call, tell us your street and the nearest cross road so we can give an honest arrival window.

If you want to talk through a problem before you decide to book, call (888) 910-8644 and ask for advice. We are glad to help you figure out whether it is an emergency or can wait for a scheduled visit.

Outside the list

If you live in Greenville, Williamston or another nearby community not on the list, call and ask. We tell you honestly whether we can reach you in a reasonable time. We do not promise coverage we cannot staff.

Where the service area facts come from

County, ZIP and population facts come from the U.S. Census 2020 count and the U.S. Postal Service ZIP list, and utility facts come from each water system's own quality report, measured against federal drinking water standards.
