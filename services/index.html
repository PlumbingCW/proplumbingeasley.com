How to reach us about any service

Every service page has the same phone number, (888) 910-8644, and the line is answered at any hour. Describe what you see, and we will tell you which service fits and what to do first.

How to choose a service

If water is flooding, a drain has stopped or the tank has failed, start with the emergency pages: burst pipe repair, drain cleaning and water heater repair. If you are planning a project, start with plumbing installation or water heater installation.

If you are not sure what is wrong, leak detection is a good starting point for hidden water, and sewer inspection shows what a camera sees in a main line.

Standards behind our service pages

Each service page cites the codes and standards that apply to that work, including the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by the South Carolina Building Codes Council and the ANSI, ASTM and NSF listings for parts.
