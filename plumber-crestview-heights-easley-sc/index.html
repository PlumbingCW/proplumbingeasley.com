A note on the location

Crestview Heights is listed in the 29642 ZIP code, the same ZIP code used by Powdersville. A shared ZIP code does not make two places the same. Powdersville is a separate area, and we cover it on its own page, plumber in Powdersville.

Listings place Crestview Heights near downtown Easley and Highway 123. We could not confirm an official boundary, so we describe it by streets: McArthur Street, Kennedy Street, Eisenhower Street, Truman Street, Roosevelt Drive, Belmont Circle, Biltmore Circle and Astor Street.

What the homes look like

Listing data shows homes mostly from the 1960s to the 1980s, with a median near 1971. Mid-century brick ranches are common, often with hardwood floors, fireplaces, garages or carports and detached storage buildings. Many lots are larger than typical for an established neighborhood.

Homes of this style are often kept up room by room. A bathroom gets updated, then a kitchen, and the plumbing behind them is touched along the way. That makes fixture work the most common reason to call.

Repair or replace a fixture?

A repair is usually right when the fixture is sound and one part has worn out, like a flapper, a cartridge or a supply line. Replacement makes sense when the body is cracked, parts are no longer available or you are already remodeling the room.

We tell you which situation you are in and give the price for both when both are reasonable. You decide, and work starts only after you approve the scope.

Before you call

  • Take a photo of the leak or the fixture label.
  • Note when the problem started.
  • Close the shutoff under the sink or behind the toilet if water is moving.
  • Keep a bucket and towels under a slow drip.
  • Have your street and the nearest cross street ready.
