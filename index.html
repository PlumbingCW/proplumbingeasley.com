Emergency plumber services in Easley, SC

An emergency plumber is a plumber who can come the same day, at any hour, to a problem that cannot wait. In Easley that usually means a burst pipe, a sewer backup, a water heater that is leaking or a leak you cannot find. Our 24/7 line is built for those calls.

Start with the urgent pages when you have a live problem: burst pipe repair, drain cleaning, water heater repair and sewer line repair. Each one tells you what to do before we arrive.

What to do in the first ten minutes

Before the plumber arrives

.

These steps work for most plumbing emergencies.

  1. Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. Stop using drains and toilets if a backup has started.
  3. Switch off the water heater if the tank is leaking or the lines are draining.
  4. Move rugs and boxes away from the wet area.
  5. Call (888) 910-8644 and give your address and what you see.

Water, houses and plumbing in Easley

Easley Combined Utilities draws water from Saluda Lake, and its 2023 report lists average hardness at 6 mg/L with a pH near 7.2. Soft water is gentle on fixtures, so most Easley plumbing problems come from age, roots and freezes, not scale.

The city grew around a railroad and textile mills. Older streets near downtown and Pendleton Street often have older pipe, while newer subdivisions have PEX. Roots in older sewer lines and galvanized supply pipe are common reasons to call.
