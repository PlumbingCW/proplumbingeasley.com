Call recording notice

Calls to (888) 910-8644 may be recorded and monitored for quality, training and to keep an accurate record of what was agreed. By continuing the call, you consent to recording. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start and we will help in another way.

Information we collect

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, service address and a description of the problem. We use it to dispatch a plumber, give you a price and keep a record of the work. We do not collect payment card numbers through this website.

Like most websites, ours may record basic technical data such as browser type and pages viewed. We use it to keep the site working. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

How we use and share information

We use your information to run dispatch and complete the job. We may share it with the plumber assigned to your call and with service providers who help us operate the phone line. We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information when the law requires it or to protect safety.

You can ask what we have on file, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal record-keeping rules. Email contact@proplumbingeasley.com or write to Easley Pro Plumbing, 407 Ross Ave, Easley, SC 29640.
